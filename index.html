why it's different

Not another chat window bolted onto your IDE.

Shellmate is built around the one interface every developer already trusts: the command line.

01

Full repo context

Indexes your working tree on launch, so every answer already knows your file structure, dependencies, and recent history.

02

Nothing runs without asking

Every suggested command shows its full effect before execution. Nothing writes to disk or runs remotely without your confirmation.

03

Bring your own model

Point it at a local model or any API-compatible provider. Your code only leaves your machine if you choose a remote one.

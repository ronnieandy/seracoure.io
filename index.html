The Story

How Seracoure came to be.

Ron Anand founded Seracoure after years in professional services where he observed a persistent problem: clients hired senior advisors and got junior delivery. The expertise that won the engagement rarely showed up in the work.

Seracoure was built to solve that. It's a deliberately small, deliberately personal practice. Ron takes on a limited number of clients at any time, ensuring that every organisation he works with receives his full attention throughout.

Clients range from founder-led businesses navigating their first major growth phase to established organisations needing an outside perspective on a specific strategic challenge.

FAQs

Common questions

How does Seracoure differ from a consultancy?+
The main difference is delivery. Traditional consultancies staff projects with teams of mixed seniority. At Seracoure, Ron is the only person who works on your engagement — every piece of thinking, advice, and deliverable comes from him directly.
What types of clients does Ron work with?+
From founder-led businesses in early growth phases to established companies facing a specific strategic challenge. What matters more than size or sector is that the client values independent thinking and direct engagement.
How are engagements structured?+
Some engagements are project-based with a defined scope and timeline. Others are ongoing advisory relationships — monthly retainers where Ron serves as a regular thinking partner. He'll propose the structure that makes most sense for your situation.
How many clients does Ron take on at once?+
Deliberately few. The whole model is built around giving clients proper attention. If Ron is at capacity, he'll say so clearly and give you a realistic timeframe.
